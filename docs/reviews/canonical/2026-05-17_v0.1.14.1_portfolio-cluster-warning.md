# Portfolio Cluster Warning (v0.1.14.1)

## Context

After v0.1.13.3 round-trip backtest delivered ✓✓ STRONG PASS (OOS net PF 2.50,
mean +1.99%), user proposed direct path to paper trading. Reviewer pushed back hard.

## Key reviewer quote

> "trade-level metrics ≠ portfolio-level deployability. ETF + 金融 已經很明顯,
> real portfolio drawdown 可能是 trade-level worst 的 2-3 倍. 直接 paper trade
> 沒有 portfolio brain = unconstrained signal stream 搬到假帳戶, 學到的東西會失真.
>
> 現在 signals 仍然高度 correlated. ETF + 金融 已經很明顯. 這意味著真實 portfolio
> drawdown 可能比 trade-level metrics 差. 所以下一步不要再加 strategy, 而是
> 直接進 Portfolio Layer."

## Decision triggered

- v0.1.14.1 portfolio layer built BEFORE paper trading
- `portfolio/risk_budget.py`, `portfolio/selector.py`, `backtest/portfolio_simulator.py`
- Sector classification, ETF cap, sector cap, cash buffer constraints
- Re-validated alpha under constraints

## Empirical validation

Reviewer prediction was 100% confirmed:
- Unconstrained backtest: 132 trades
- Constrained backtest: 72 trades (45% rejected — many would have been clustered)
- Reject distribution: symbol_already_held 29.7%, etf_cap 24.1%, sector_cap_financial
  15.9% — exactly the cluster patterns reviewer warned about

## Bonus discovery (later)

Reviewer warned "max DD may be 2-3x trade-level worst". Actual outcome:
- Trade-level worst: -7.73%
- Portfolio max DD (constrained): -11.01% (1.4x ratio — much less than feared)

Why? Because sector caps actively prevented the simultaneous loading reviewer was
worried about. The constraint did its job. This insight became §7.5 Portfolio
Philosophy ("constraints are signal filters, not just risk caps").

## Status: canonical

This review is the origin of ADR-003 and §7.5. It remains consulted whenever
portfolio-layer design questions arise.
