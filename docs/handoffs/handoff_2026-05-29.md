## 7. Lessons Learned

1. **Per-horizon spacing is critical.** Global spacing=20 severely under-sampled 5d horizon and produced artifact findings (absorption bear alpha, slope trap). Always use per-horizon spacing.

2. **Absorption finding was overturned.** The +0.94% lift in bear regime did not survive the spacing fix. Absolute returns turned negative for both has_abs and no_abs groups. The relative advantage remains but is not actionable.

3. **RS_T3 + dist < 0 is the most robust finding.** Survived spacing fix, strengthened from +1.07% to +1.66% lift. Consistent across all horizons. Regime-dependent (works in bull/crisis, fails in bear).

4. **Distance/slope/spread are mostly RS proxies.** Not independent alpha sources. Their value is in entry timing (dist < 0 = pullback) and trap avoidance (dead zone 0-2 ATR), not standalone ranking.

5. **Beta_T3 + RS_T3 combination is consistently the strongest cell** regardless of other features. High beta high RS stocks pulled back to MA20 = best entry.
